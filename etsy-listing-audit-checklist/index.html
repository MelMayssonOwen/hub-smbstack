How do you audit an Etsy listing for SEO? (8-point checklist)

Direct answer

Audit eight things, in order: (1) the title front-loads your main keyword phrase; (2) all 13 tags are used with multi-word phrases; (3) the first image stops the scroll; (4) attributes and category are fully filled; (5) the description opens with the essentials; (6) price and shipping are competitive (shipping affects placement); (7) you have matched real buyer search terms, not just your own words; (8) you track conversion, because clicks that don't convert hurt ranking. The 8-point Etsy listing audit

  1. Title: lead with the exact phrase a buyer would type. Etsy reads the front of the title heavily; put the strongest keyword first, then variations, kept readable for humans.
  2. All 13 tags: use every tag, each a multi-word phrase (e.g. "personalised dog collar", not "dog"). Cover synonyms and long-tail variants rather than repeating one phrase.
  3. First image: the thumbnail decides the click. A clear, well-lit, scroll-stopping first photo raises click-through, which feeds ranking.
  4. Attributes & category: fill every attribute and pick the most specific category, Etsy uses these as ranking and filter signals.
  5. Description: put the key details in the first line or two; it helps buyers and increasingly feeds search and off-Etsy surfaces.
  6. Price & shipping: competitive pricing and, especially, free/low shipping influence placement and conversion. Model your shipping against category norms.
  7. Buyer language: validate your keywords against how buyers actually search (Etsy search autocomplete + a research tool), not just how you describe the product.
  8. Conversion: a listing that gets clicks but no sales gets suppressed. Watch views-to-sales and iterate the photo, price and title.

What Etsy actually rewards

Per Etsy's Seller Handbook, search combines relevance (how well tags and title match the query), listing quality/conversion, recency (a minor factor), shipping price, and customer/market experience signals. Chasing relisting tricks over relevance and conversion is a common waste of time, Etsy has downplayed manual renewal as a ranking lever. Fix the eight items above and let conversion compound.

Frequently asked questions

How do I audit an Etsy listing for SEO?
Check eight things: the title front-loads your main keyword, all 13 tags are used with multi-word phrases, the first image is scroll-stopping, attributes and category are complete, the description opens with the key info, price and shipping are competitive, renewal/recency is considered, and the listing matches real buyer search terms. Etsy's Seller Handbook is the primary source for how each factor affects search.
How many tags should an Etsy listing use?
All 13. Etsy gives every listing 13 tags and unused tags are wasted ranking surface. Use multi-word phrases rather than single words, and avoid repeating the exact same phrase across tags when you could cover more variations.
Does relisting improve Etsy SEO?
Etsy has said recency is a minor factor and that manually renewing listings is not a reliable ranking strategy on its own; focus on relevance, tags, quality score and conversion first. Check the Seller Handbook for Etsy's current guidance rather than relying on relisting folklore.
