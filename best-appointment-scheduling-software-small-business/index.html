What is the best appointment scheduling software for a small business in 2026?

Direct answer

For most small service businesses, Square Appointments is the best all-in-one pick because its scheduling tier is free for a solo pro and it bundles payments and reminders; Calendly is best for meeting-style booking, cal.com for teams that want an open-source, self-hostable option, and Setmore for the cheapest multi-staff plan (free for up to 4 users). If cutting no-shows is the specific goal, choose the tool with the strongest reminder-plus-deposit workflow rather than the longest feature list.

How to choose in one minute

  • You take payment at the appointment (salon, clinic, coaching): Square Appointments or Setmore Pro, native deposits and card-on-file.
  • You book calls, demos or consults: Calendly (polished, widely recognised) or cal.com (open source, self-hostable, cheaper at team scale).
  • You run classes or need detailed intake forms: Acuity.
  • Your real problem is no-shows: prioritise deposits + confirmations over feature count, see the section below.

The real question behind scheduling software: no-shows

Most small businesses shop for scheduling software because missed appointments cost them money. Reviews of appointment no-shows across healthcare consistently report no-show rates in the double digits (commonly cited in the 10 to 30% range depending on specialty and reminder practice); you can browse the underlying studies via PubMed's no-show research index. The two interventions with the strongest evidence are automated reminders/confirmations and requiring a deposit or card on file. Every tool above sends reminders; deposits are where they differ. If deposits and confirmations are the whole point for you, a tool built around that job can matter more than the broader calendar.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best free appointment scheduling software for a small business?
For a solo operator, Square Appointments and Setmore both have genuinely free tiers that take bookings and send reminders; Calendly's free plan is strong for one meeting type. The 'best' free tool depends on whether you need payments (Square), multiple staff (Setmore, up to 4 users free) or calendar-only meeting booking (Calendly, cal.com).
How much does appointment scheduling software cost?
Entry paid tiers cluster around $5 to $16 per user per month billed annually: Setmore Pro is $5/user/mo, Calendly Standard is $10/seat/mo and Teams $16/seat/mo, cal.com Teams is $12/user/mo. Square Appointments and Acuity add payment processing and start free or low but charge card fees. Prices verified from each vendor's pricing page, August 2026.
Does scheduling software reduce no-shows?
Indirectly. Automated SMS/email reminders and required deposits are the two mechanisms shown to cut no-shows; most scheduling tools include reminders, and some add deposits or card-on-file. Tools built specifically around no-show reduction lean harder on confirmations and deposit holds. See our no-show guide for the evidence.
Do I need scheduling software with payments built in?
If you take payment at or before the appointment (salons, clinics, coaching), a tool with native payments or deposits removes a manual step and discourages no-shows. If you only need to book meetings, a calendar-first tool like Calendly or cal.com is simpler and cheaper.
