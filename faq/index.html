Frequently asked questions

How does SMB Stack Brief make money?
SMB Stack Brief is published by Opsibyte, which operates four SMB tools (NoShowLine, Listingtonic, TaskDrain, BreachTrigger). Those products are disclosed and clearly labelled wherever they appear and are compared on the same terms as competitors; links to them carry a sponsored/nofollow tag. We prioritise being useful and accurate over promoting our own tools, and most links on every page point to third-party primary sources, not to us.
Are your prices up to date?
Prices are taken from each vendor's public pricing page on the date shown (most verified August 2026) and linked so you can re-check. Where a vendor sells only via quotes or resellers, we say so instead of inventing a figure. Software pricing changes; always confirm on the vendor's own page before buying.
Why do some tools show 'no public price'?
Several enterprise-leaning vendors, for example Dark Web ID, SpyCloud and Flare in breach monitoring, do not publish prices and route buyers through sales or resellers. Rather than guess, we mark that plainly. It is itself useful information: transparent, self-serve pricing is an advantage for a small business with no procurement team.
Is the best appointment scheduling software free?
Several strong options have genuine free tiers: Square Appointments (solo), Setmore (up to 4 users), Calendly (one event type) and cal.com (open source). Free is often enough to start; paid tiers add staff, payments/deposits and advanced rules. See the scheduling guide.
Does dark-web monitoring remove my leaked data?
No. Monitoring is early warning only, it tells you a credential appeared in a breach so you can change it and enable MFA. Nothing removes data already circulating. Be wary of any service implying otherwise.
Do I need both Helium 10 and Jungle Scout?
Almost never, they overlap heavily. Pick one Amazon suite. See the Helium 10 vs Jungle Scout comparison.
