How we compare things

We start from the buyer's question, not the vendor's feature list. Prices are taken from each vendor's own public pricing page on the date shown and linked so you can re-check them; where a vendor sells only through quotes or resellers, we say so rather than invent a number. Statistics are tied to a named primary source, CISA, the FTC, NIST, PubMed, the SEC, or the marketplaces' own seller documentation. See our methodology and disclosures.
