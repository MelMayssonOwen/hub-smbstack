What is the best Etsy SEO tool for a small shop in 2026?

Direct answer

eRank is the best all-round Etsy SEO tool for most sellers: a real free tier, then paid plans from $5.99/month, covering keyword research, competition and listing audits. Marmalead ($19/month) is the pick for deep keyword brainstorming; Alura and EverBee bundle product research with SEO and both have free tiers. Start on eRank's free plan and pay only when its limits bite. Whatever you use, ground your keywords in Etsy's own Seller Handbook guidance on how search works.

eRank, the default first tool

eRank covers the Etsy-SEO basics, keyword research, keyword trends, competitor and listing audits, and its free tier is enough to start. Paid plans are transparent: Basic $5.99/month, Pro $9.99/month, Expert $29.99/month (a little less billed annually). For most small shops it is the right first purchase, or the only one.

Marmalead, for keyword depth

Marmalead is pricier at $19/month (or $16/month annual, with a one-time $300 lifetime option) and skips a permanent free tier, but sellers who do heavy keyword work value its brainstorming and letter-grade scoring. Choose it if research is a daily activity, not an occasional one.

Alura & EverBee, research plus SEO

Alura and EverBee blend Etsy product/market research (estimated sales and revenue) with keyword tools, and both offer limited free tiers. They are worth a look if you want product opportunity data alongside SEO rather than a pure keyword tool. Neither publishes a single flat headline price we could verify cleanly, so confirm current tiers on their sites.

Research tells you the keywords, something has to write the listing

Every tool above is fundamentally a research tool: it surfaces terms and gaps. Turning that into a strong title, 13 tags and a description that reads well is a separate step. Some sellers do it by hand from the research; others use a rewrite/optimization tool. Either way, anchor the work in Etsy's Seller Handbook on how search and relevancy actually work, the primary source the tools are all interpreting.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best Etsy SEO tool in 2026?
eRank is the best value for most Etsy sellers: it has a free tier and paid plans from $5.99/month, covering keyword research, competition and listing audits. Marmalead ($19/month) is favoured for keyword depth and 'storm' brainstorming, while Alura and EverBee bundle product research with SEO. Start on eRank's free tier and upgrade only when you hit its limits.
Is eRank or Marmalead better for Etsy?
eRank is cheaper and has a free tier, making it the default first tool; Marmalead costs more ($19/month vs eRank's $5.99 Basic) but many sellers prefer its keyword-brainstorming and grading. If budget matters, start with eRank; if you live in keyword research all day, Marmalead's depth can justify the price.
Do I need a paid Etsy SEO tool at all?
Not to start. Etsy's own Seller Handbook explains how search works and eRank's free tier covers basic keyword research. A paid tool pays off once you have enough listings that manual research is the bottleneck, or you want competition and trend data the free tiers cap.
What is the difference between keyword tools and listing-optimization tools?
Keyword-research tools (eRank, Marmalead) tell you which terms to target; listing-optimization tools focus on turning that research into the actual title, tags and description. Many sellers use a research tool to find terms, then write or rewrite the listing themselves or with an optimization tool.
