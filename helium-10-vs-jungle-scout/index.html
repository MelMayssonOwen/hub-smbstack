Helium 10 vs Jungle Scout: which Amazon seller tool should you use?

Direct answer

Both are full Amazon-seller suites and most sellers only need one. Helium 10 is the pick for transparent pricing and breadth, a limited free tier, then Platinum at $99/month (annual), while Jungle Scout is chosen for its product-research database and sales estimates. Run one, not both; they overlap on product research, keywords and listing tools. Helium 10

Helium 10 spans product research (Black Box), keyword research (Cerebro, Magnet), listing optimization and operations. It publishes its pricing clearly: a limited free tier, then Platinum $99/month (annual) or $129 monthly, Diamond $279/month (annual) or $359 monthly, and Enterprise from $1,499/month. The transparency and breadth make it the common default.

Jungle Scout

Jungle Scout built its reputation on product-research data, sales estimates and opportunity scoring, via its Catalyst plans for sellers under ~$1M revenue and Cobalt for brands above. Its main pricing page shows plan structure and a verified $49/month per additional seat, but routes exact tier prices to a dedicated page, so confirm current rates there. Choose it if its product database and estimates match how you source products.

The honest verdict: pick one

These suites overlap so heavily that paying for both wastes money. Decide on your primary job, product sourcing vs keyword/listing work, try the free/guarantee entry of each, and commit to one. Whatever you pick, remember tools find opportunities; Amazon still decides rankings on relevance and conversion, which is why testing changes in Amazon's Manage Your Experiments beats trusting a ranking promise.

Frequently asked questions

Helium 10 or Jungle Scout, which should I use?
Both are full Amazon-seller suites covering product research, keywords and listing tools. Helium 10 publishes pricing (Platinum $99/month annual, Diamond $279/month annual) and has a limited free tier; Jungle Scout is known for product-research data accuracy but shows less public flat pricing on its main page. Pick Helium 10 for transparent pricing and breadth; pick Jungle Scout if its product database and estimates fit your workflow. Most sellers do not need both.
Do I need both Helium 10 and Jungle Scout?
Almost never. They overlap heavily, product research, keyword tools, listing optimization. Running both duplicates cost. Pick one suite, learn it well, and only add a second specialised tool if you have a specific gap.
How much does Helium 10 cost?
Helium 10 has a limited free tier, then Platinum at $99/month (billed yearly) or $129/month monthly, Diamond at $279/month yearly ($359 monthly), and Enterprise from $1,499/month, verified from Helium 10's pricing page, August 2026.
Do Amazon listing tools guarantee higher rankings?
No. They surface keywords, competition and A/B-test opportunities, but rankings depend on relevance, conversion and sales velocity that Amazon controls. Use Amazon's own Manage Your Experiments to test listing changes and measure real lift rather than trusting any guarantee.
