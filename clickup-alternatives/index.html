What are the best ClickUp alternatives for a small team?

Direct answer

Asana (Starter $10.99/user/month annual) is the alternative for teams that want structured projects with less configuration; Todoist (free tier) for fast, lightweight task lists; Trello (free tier) for a simple kanban board. The most common reason to leave ClickUp is that its flexibility becomes complexity the team won't adopt, so the best alternative is usually the simpler tool people will actually open.

Asana, structure without the complexity

Asana offers clear projects, tasks and timelines that teams adopt quickly, with a free Personal tier and Starter at $10.99/user/month (annual). It is the pick when you want ClickUp-style project tracking but less to configure.

Todoist, the lightweight swap

Todoist strips things back to fast task lists with a clean free tier and low-cost paid plans. Choose it when ClickUp feels like overkill for what is really a shared to-do list.

Trello, the simplest board

Trello's kanban boards are the easiest thing to adopt: columns and cards, free to start. It is the right alternative when a visual board is all the process your team needs.

The real test: adoption

The best task tool is the one your team opens every day. If ClickUp sits unused because it's overwhelming, a simpler tool is an upgrade, not a downgrade. Once tasks have a reliable home, the higher-leverage move is eliminating the repetitive work itself.

TaskDrain sits alongside these rather than competing with them: it audits your workflows to find busywork worth automating, and is not a task manager. For tracking tasks, pick from the alternatives above.

Frequently asked questions

What are the best ClickUp alternatives?
Asana (structured projects, Starter $10.99/user/month annual), Todoist (lightweight lists, free tier), and Trello (simple kanban, free tier) are the main alternatives. Choose Asana for simpler structured project tracking, Todoist for fast task capture, and Trello for a pure visual board. All three have free tiers to trial.
Is there a simpler alternative to ClickUp?
Yes, many teams find ClickUp too configurable. Trello (kanban boards) and Todoist (task lists) are markedly simpler and both free to start. Asana sits in between: more structure than Trello, less complexity than ClickUp.
Why switch away from ClickUp?
Usually because its flexibility becomes complexity the team won't adopt, or you want a simpler board/list. If your team uses ClickUp's power, there is little reason to move; if it sits unused because it's overwhelming, a simpler tool that people actually open is the better choice.
