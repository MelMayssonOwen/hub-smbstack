Calendly vs Square Appointments vs Setmore: which fits a small business?

Direct answer

Square Appointments wins for businesses that take payment at the appointment (free for a solo pro, native card processing and deposits). Calendly wins for booking meetings, demos and calls (the most polished calendar-scheduling experience). Setmore wins on price for a small team — free for up to 4 users, then $5/user/month. They are three different jobs, so the answer is driven by whether you sell time slots, meetings, or appointments with payment.

Calendly

Calendly is meeting-scheduling first: share a link, invitees pick a slot, it lands on your calendar. The free plan covers one event type; Standard is $10/seat/month and Teams $16/seat/month billed annually ($12 and $20 billed monthly), and Enterprise starts around $15k/year. Payments come through integrations rather than natively, which is the main gap for appointment-and-pay businesses.

Square Appointments

Square Appointments is booking bolted onto Square's point-of-sale, so payments, deposits and card-on-file are native. A solo operator can use it free (you still pay standard Square card fees); Plus and Premium are paid monthly tiers for multi-staff and advanced rules — Square does not publish a flat headline monthly figure on the page we checked, so confirm the current rate on their site.

Setmore

Setmore is the value pick for a small team: free for up to 4 users, then Pro at $5/user/month billed annually, with SMS reminders, two-way calendar sync and native payments on the paid tier. It is the cheapest way to give several staff their own bookable calendars.

Frequently asked questions

Is Calendly or Square Appointments better for a small business?
Choose Square Appointments if you take payment at the appointment and want a free solo plan with built-in card processing; choose Calendly if your job is booking meetings, calls or demos into your calendar. Square is a point-of-sale-first booking tool; Calendly is a meeting-scheduling-first tool.
What is the cheapest of Calendly, Square and Setmore?
Setmore is the cheapest paid multi-staff plan at $5/user/month (annual), and it is free for up to 4 users. Square Appointments is free for a solo operator (you pay card processing fees). Calendly's paid Standard plan is $10/seat/month (annual).
Does Square Appointments charge per booking?
Square does not charge a per-booking scheduling fee on its free tier, but it takes standard Square card-processing fees when you accept payment, and its Plus/Premium tiers are monthly subscriptions. Check Square's pricing page for current processing rates.
