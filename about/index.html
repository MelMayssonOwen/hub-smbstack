About SMB Stack Brief

Direct answer

SMB Stack Brief is an independent buyer-research desk covering four kinds of software small businesses shop for: appointment scheduling, breach/dark-web monitoring, marketplace listing tools, and task management. We lead every page with a direct answer, show real published pricing (or say plainly when a vendor won't publish one), and cite primary sources. We are published by Opsibyte, which also makes four SMB tools — disclosed and labelled wherever they appear.

Updated August 2026 · Reviewed by the SMB Stack Brief desk

How we research

  • Pricing is verified, dated and linked. We take prices from each vendor's own public pricing page and link it so you can re-check. Most figures on this site were verified in August 2026. Software pricing moves — always confirm on the vendor's page before buying.
  • We don't invent numbers. When a vendor sells only via quotes or resellers (common in enterprise security), we say "no public price" rather than estimate. That absence is itself useful buyer information.
  • Statistics are tied to named primary sources — CISA, the FTC, NIST, PubMed, Etsy's Seller Handbook, Amazon Seller Central and vendor documentation — with real links, not paraphrased claims.
  • We answer the buyer's question first. Each page opens with the direct answer, then the evidence, then the caveats.

Ownership & disclosure

SMB Stack Brief is published by Opsibyte, which operates four small-business tools:

  • NoShowLine — appointment confirmations and deposit holds to reduce no-shows (mentioned on scheduling pages).
  • Listingtonic — AI Amazon listing audit (mentioned on listing pages; it is Amazon-only, not an Etsy tool).
  • TaskDrain — an automation audit that finds busywork to automate (mentioned on productivity pages; not a task manager).
  • BreachTrigger — SEC EDGAR 8-K material-incident alerts (mentioned on security pages; it is not a credential/dark-web monitor and not a HIBP alternative).

Wherever one of these appears, it is clearly labelled as our own product, compared on the same terms as competitors, and linked with a sponsored/nofollow tag. We hold ourselves to describing each accurately — including saying what it is not. On every page, the majority of outbound links point to independent primary sources, not to us.

Corrections

Found an out-of-date price or an error? Prices change and we'd rather fix it than mislead a buyer. The vendor's own linked pricing page is always the authority; treat this site as a starting map, and verify the specifics before you purchase.
