What are the best eRank alternatives for Etsy sellers?

Direct answer

Marmalead ($19/month) is the main paid alternative for deeper keyword research; Alura and EverBee add product-research and sales estimates alongside SEO and both have free tiers. If you have moved to Amazon, the equivalent suites are Helium 10 and Jungle Scout. But note eRank already has a free tier and $5.99/month Basic, so most 'alternatives' are step-ups in depth, not cheaper swaps.

Marmalead — the keyword-depth swap

Marmalead costs more than eRank ($19/month, or $16/month annual) and lacks a permanent free tier, but its keyword brainstorming and letter-grade scoring appeal to sellers who do heavy research. It is a depth upgrade, not a cost saving.

Alura & EverBee — research-plus-SEO swaps

Alura and EverBee fold Etsy product/market research and sales estimates into their SEO tooling and both have limited free tiers, so you can trial them at no cost. Pick them if you want opportunity data alongside keywords rather than a pure keyword tool.

Helium 10 / Jungle Scout — the Amazon swap

If the reason you are leaving eRank is that you have moved to Amazon, the relevant tools are the Amazon suites, not another Etsy tool — see our Helium 10 vs Jungle Scout comparison.

The free baseline nobody should skip

Before paying for any tool, the Etsy Seller Handbook explains how Etsy search, relevancy and quality scoring work — the primary source every paid tool is interpreting. Combined with Etsy's own search autocomplete, it is a free starting point.

Frequently asked questions

What are the best alternatives to eRank?
For Etsy, Marmalead ($19/month) is the main paid alternative for keyword depth, while Alura and EverBee bundle product research with SEO and both have free tiers. Etsy's own Seller Handbook is the free primary source behind all of them. For Amazon, the equivalent suites are Helium 10 and Jungle Scout.
Is there a free alternative to eRank?
eRank already has a free tier, which is the cheapest option. Alura and EverBee also offer limited free tiers. For zero-cost keyword ideas, Etsy's own search bar autocomplete and the Seller Handbook guidance are free primary sources.
Why switch from eRank?
Usually for deeper keyword tooling (Marmalead), bundled product-research/sales estimates (Alura, EverBee), or because you sell on Amazon rather than Etsy (Helium 10, Jungle Scout). If eRank's free or Basic tier meets your needs, there is little reason to switch.
